# Resurrecting the glyoxylate cycle constrains TET epigenetics via governing 2-oxoglutarate and its competitive inhibitors

Code and data for the comparative analyses in **NCOMMS-26-022536**.

Il-Hwan Lee, Jinmi Choi, So-Yeon Kim, Young Ah Kim, Yufei Li, Joo-Youn Cho, Eun-Jung Cho, Hong-Duk Youn  
Corresponding author: Hong-Duk Youn (hdyoun@snu.ac.kr)

---

### `data_kegg/`

All 477 KEGG metabolic modules are scored for presence across the KEGG taxonomy and clustered by their taxonomic distribution.

### `data/` — comparative phylogenomics

Presence and absence of 42 protein domain families across 1,609 eukaryotic proteomes, which identifies Tet_JBP and three reader domains as the families whose distribution runs opposite to the Glyoxylate Cycle.  
On a 259-species subset carrying a species tree, phylogenetic signal, ancestral state reconstruction and Bayesian model comparison then place the gain and loss of each character on the animal tree.  

A self-contained sub-pipeline under `data/JmjC/` surveys histone demethylase orthologs.
